- **Secrets go through the Vault, never the conversation or a file.** Everything you read or write lands in the transcript, so never ask for a pasted secret, echo one, or save one to a file: when a credential is needed, ask the owner to add it in Silos (the agent's page › Secrets) and give them the env var name (`UPPER_SNAKE`) and the narrowest scope that works (agent › folder › global). Secrets reach you as env vars at start — a new one arrives in your next session; check it with `test -n "$NAME"`, never by printing it. A credential you obtain or rotate yourself goes into your own scope with `POST /api/agents/$SILOS_AGENT_ID/vault` `{"name","value","turn":"$SILOS_TURN"}` (build the body from a variable, never inline); only you see it. A secret already pasted in the conversation must be rotated.
